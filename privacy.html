PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 29, 2026

Your reasons
are yours.

ReasonGate is developed by John Barney. It helps you manage your own device without sending your essays or app activity to us.

Essay text

Essays remain in memory while the editor is open and are discarded when it closes. They are never saved by ReasonGate or uploaded to the developer.

Data on your device

Rules, private app-selection tokens, and up to 100 unlock timestamps, word counts, rule names, and exception durations are stored on your device and shared with ReasonGate’s Screen Time extensions. These records may be included in your device backups. Clear unlock history in Settings and complete a rule’s required essay to delete it and remove its saved selections.

Apple services

Apple handles Screen Time authorization, the system’s website filtering, subscription payments, and purchase restoration. ReasonGate checks Apple-verified subscription status to enable paid features. The developer does not receive your payment-card information.

No account, advertising, or tracking

This release has no app accounts, parent passkeys, child sharing, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. The app does not send essays, app selections, browsing history, or device usage to a developer server.

This support website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or third-party fonts.

Optional support requests

If you choose to create a GitHub support issue, your GitHub username and the content you post are public. The developer uses that information to respond to your request. Posting is optional and is not required to use the app. Do not post sensitive information. You can edit or remove your own issue content through GitHub’s controls, subject to GitHub’s retention policies.

Your choices and contact

Delete local rules after completing their required essays; clear unlock history in Settings. Revoke individual Screen Time authorization through iOS Settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account; deleting ReasonGate does not cancel a subscription.

For support or privacy questions, contact John Barney through ReasonGate support issues. Keep public requests free of personal data. Material policy changes will be posted here with an updated date.
