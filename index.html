GETTING STARTED

A few choices. A clearer day.

1. Subscribe from Home

Tap Subscribe to ReasonGate Plus on the Focus screen, or restore an existing purchase. Free download; blocking features require US $4.99/month or $39.99/year. Both plans include all features; no trial. Apple shows your local price before you confirm.

2. Connect Screen Time

After your subscription is active, Connect Screen Time appears on the Focus screen. Approve Apple’s individual authorization, then choose apps using Apple’s private picker. No Screen Time permission is requested before subscription.

3. Create a rule

Choose apps, categories, or websites. Block your selections, or allow only selected individual apps and websites. Add allowed hours or a shared daily budget. Creating a rule needs no essay.

HELP & TROUBLESHOOTING

A boundary you understand.

How do essay exceptions work?

When an app is shielded, close it and open ReasonGate from the Home Screen. Tap Write my reason on an eligible rule. Type the selected 25–1,000 words, or 50–2,000 characters when creating rules in Chinese, Japanese, or Thai, then complete the essay to lift that whole rule for 15–60 minutes. For an exception covering only one app, create a one-app rule. Other rules and the adult website filter still apply.

Copy, paste, drag-and-drop, dictation results, and Writing Tools are disabled in the editor. Letters and numbers count toward character limits; spaces, punctuation, and emoji do not. Language changes do not change saved requirements. The app checks length, not meaning, and cannot reliably detect individual keystroke automation. Essay text is discarded when the editor closes.

Can I turn off or delete a rule without an essay?

Creating a rule needs no essay. Once saved, it is locked and cannot be edited or switched off. Taking a timed break or deleting it requires a freshly typed essay at its saved length and counting unit. To change a rule, delete it with its essay and create a replacement. Cancelling or closing the essay leaves the rule unchanged. Rules with timed breaks disabled still require an essay for deletion.

Choose a separate removal essay of 25–1,000 words (50–2,000 characters for Chinese, Japanese, or Thai) before enabling the adult website filter. The saved length and counting unit stay locked while protection is on. Turning the filter off requires that essay. App-rule breaks do not turn it off. iOS still lets the device owner revoke individual Screen Time permission outside ReasonGate.

How do schedules and daily budgets work?

Allowed hours repeat every day in your device’s local time and can cross midnight. Both the allowed and blocked intervals must be at least 15 minutes. Daily budgets apply to all selections in one rule together and include earlier activity on this phone today. They reset at local midnight.

Keep automatic date and time enabled. Schedule changes and break endings depend on iOS Device Activity callbacks, which may be delayed. Overlapping rules combine; an essay does not bypass a separate strict rule.

What does the adult content filter cover?

In Settings, choose the Removal essay length, then turn on Block adult websites to enable Apple’s automatic website filter in supported browsing contexts. It does not inspect or guarantee removal of adult content inside social feeds, messages, or every app.

Why are apps still blocked, or no longer blocked?

Check each rule for overlapping restrictions, its hours and remaining exception. If Screen Time permission was revoked, reconnect, delete affected rules with their essays, and create replacements using Apple’s picker. Apple invalidates the previous selection tokens. You can revoke ReasonGate’s individual Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings for recovery.

ReasonGate is a personal focus tool. Saved rules are read-only, and breaks or deletion require an essay; iOS still lets you revoke permission. This release does not include child profiles, sharing, or parent passkeys.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that purchased the plan. Use Settings → Manage subscription to change or cancel it. Existing protections remain after expiration, and you can delete rules or turn off the filter without paying, after completing the required essay. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

What stays on my device?

Rules, app-selection tokens, and up to 100 recent unlock records stay on your device and may be included in device backups. Essays are not saved or uploaded. No app account is required, and there are no ads or analytics SDKs. Read the privacy policy.
