Billing and renewal

Prices above are the intended US prices. Apple displays your current local price before you confirm a purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage, restore, or cancel

Use Settings → Manage subscription in ReasonGate or your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore purchases from ReasonGate Settings using the purchasing Apple Account. Existing rules remain after access expires; you can remove rules and disable the website filter without paying. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
